Frequently asked questions

Can I move to a bigger plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade from Starter through to Dedicated Core at any time. Resources are added to your existing server, so there’s no need to reinstall or migrate your data.

What happens when my annual term ends?

Your server renews automatically at the same annual rate unless you cancel. We’ll email a reminder 14 days before renewal so there are no surprises.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

All plans include a 7-day money-back window from first activation, covering both monthly and annual billing.

Which payment methods do you accept?

See the full list in the footer below. We support local mobile wallets, bank transfer and international cards so you can pay however suits you best.

Is server management included?

Starter and Growth are self-managed with optional add-ons. Scale and Dedicated Core include priority and dedicated support respectively, covering OS-level troubleshooting.
